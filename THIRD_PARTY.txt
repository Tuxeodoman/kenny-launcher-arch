The package includes Qt 6.11.2, Eclipse Temurin OpenJDK 8/17/21/25,
7-Zip, authlib-injector and shared libraries from Ubuntu 24.04.

Qt license texts are in licenses/Qt inside the archive. Qt is dynamically
linked; its libraries can be replaced in lib/, plugins/ and qml/.
